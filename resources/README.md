# Reader Resources

This section contains supporting material for DevOps in the Trenches. Resources deepen the book without interrupting its main narrative.

## Resource Areas

### further-reading/

Curated standards, specifications, primary research, official documentation, and specialist books arranged by subject.

### frameworks/

Practical decision frameworks for production readiness, reliability, security, recovery, platform governance, incident learning, and cost control.

### templates/

Reusable records and worksheets for service ownership, architecture decisions, change risk, incidents, recovery exercises, platform reviews, and executive reporting.

### glossary/

Definitions of production-engineering terms as they are used in the book.

## Source Standard

Resources are selected in this order of preference:

1. Official standards and specifications.
2. Primary research and original technical publications.
3. Official project and product documentation.
4. Established books and professionally reviewed material.
5. Credible secondary analysis when primary material is unavailable.

Inclusion does not imply endorsement. External material remains under the control of its owner and may change.

## Using Templates

Adapt templates to your architecture, scale, risk, regulation, staffing, and business requirements. Preserve the purpose of the control even when changing its format.

Do not place credentials, personal information, customer data, confidential incidents, restricted architecture, or private company records in publicly accessible copies.

## Corrections

Report broken references, outdated standards, and factual errors through the repository issue templates. Include an official replacement source where possible.
