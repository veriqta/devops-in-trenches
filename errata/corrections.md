# Confirmed Corrections

No confirmed corrections are recorded for the current public reader edition.

Each confirmed correction will include:

- A unique correction identifier.
- The affected edition or repository version.
- The chapter, file, and section.
- A description of the error.
- The corrected guidance.
- The date confirmed.
- The version containing the correction.

To report a possible error, follow the instructions in the [errata guide](README.md).
