# Free Chapters from DevOps in the Trenches

*DevOps in the Trenches* is a production-focused field guide for engineers and technical leaders responsible for systems that must continue working while people, software, infrastructure, dependencies, and business conditions change.

These three complete sample chapters show the book from three angles: production thinking, controlled delivery, and incident leadership.

## Start Here

### 1. [Chapter 1: Production Does Not Care About Your Transformation Plan](chapter-01-production-does-not-care-about-your-transformation-plan.md)

The book's central argument. Tools, programs, and adoption milestones do not prove that production has become safer, faster, or easier to recover.

### 2. [Chapter 19: Progressive Delivery, Rollback, and Roll-Forward](chapter-19-progressive-delivery-rollback-and-roll-forward.md)

A technical chapter about controlling exposure, selecting representative canaries, governing promotion, and designing recovery before a release expands.

### 3. [Chapter 54: Incident Command and Decision-Making Under Pressure](chapter-54-incident-command-and-decision-making-under-pressure.md)

An operational leadership chapter about declaring incidents, assigning authority, controlling changes, communicating uncertainty, and closing incidents responsibly.

## Recommended Reading Order

Read Chapter 1 first. It establishes the production-first argument that governs the rest of the book. Continue to Chapter 19 for delivery judgment, then Chapter 54 for coordinated response under pressure.

The three chapters are deliberately separated across the book. Together, they show how *DevOps in the Trenches* connects organizational design, engineering practice, and production leadership.

## Who This Book Is For

- Software engineers moving into production ownership
- DevOps, platform, cloud, and infrastructure engineers
- Site reliability engineers
- Security and delivery engineers
- Engineering managers and technical leaders
- Organizations trying to improve delivery without hiding operational risk

## About the Book

*DevOps in the Trenches* contains 63 chapters across 12 parts. It is not a command reference, certification guide, or vendor manual. It examines how production systems are designed, changed, secured, recovered, funded, governed, and led when conditions are incomplete and consequences are real.
