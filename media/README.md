# Media and Press Resources

This section is the source for approved public media related to DevOps in the Trenches.

## Directories

### cover/

Approved book-cover images and authorized display versions.

### author/

Approved author photographs and biographical material.

### press-kit/

Approved book descriptions, author information, interview notes, publication facts, and media assets.

## Usage Rules

Media files may be used only for accurate editorial coverage, reviews, interviews, listings, and authorized promotion of DevOps in the Trenches.

Do not:

- Alter the cover in a way that changes its meaning or appearance.
- Remove copyright, attribution, or branding.
- Use author images to imply endorsement of another product or service.
- Place the book or author identity in misleading, unlawful, defamatory, or deceptive material.
- Sell or redistribute media assets as standalone products.
- Present an unofficial design as an approved edition.

Maintain the original proportions and reasonable image quality. Identify the book and author accurately.
