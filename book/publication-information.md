## Operational and Security Disclaimer

Production systems differ. Commands, configurations, architectural patterns, checklists, and procedures should be reviewed, tested, authorized, and approved before use.

Readers are responsible for:

- Testing changes in an appropriate environment.
- Protecting credentials, customer information, and confidential data.
- Confirming authorization before accessing or modifying systems.
- Establishing backups and verified recovery paths.
- Reviewing blast radius, failure modes, and rollback or roll-forward options.
- Following organizational policies, contracts, and applicable law.
- Obtaining required approval for security tests and resilience experiments.

Do not execute destructive commands, security tests, chaos experiments, failovers, credential rotations, recovery procedures, or production changes without authorization, safeguards, observation, and stop conditions.

The book does not authorize access to any account, system, network, repository, dataset, or service. Work only within environments you own or are explicitly permitted to use.
